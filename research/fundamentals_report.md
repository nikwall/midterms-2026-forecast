# Fundamentals: district lean on the 2026 lines plus the national environment

Generated by `pipeline/fundamentals.py` on 2026-11-02.

**In plain language.** Each seat starts from how it voted for president in 2024, measured against the country as a whole. The national mood for 2026 is added on top, plus a bonus for an incumbent who is on the ballot. No polls of the race and no market prices enter this input; it is what history and the national mood alone would predict.

**Formula.** expected margin = (2024 margin − national 2024 margin of R+1.6) + environment + incumbency (±3). The probability uses an error of √(environment sd² + race sd²), with race sd 6 for House and 16 for Senate.
