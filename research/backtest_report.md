# Backtest: calibrating the model on past elections

Generated by `pipeline/backtest.py` on 2026-10-27. Everything is evaluated as of 7 days before Election Day.

## Data

- Poll archive (FiveThirtyEight, 1998–2022): 213 Senate, 630 House-district and 7 generic-ballot poll averages in the midterms 1998, 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018, 2022.
- Senate results and presidential results by state, 1976–2024 (MIT Election Data + Science Lab): 239 contested Senate midterm races with a lean.
- Polymarket 2024 Senate prices on 29 Oct 2024: 5 races (5 not safe, priced 5–95%).
- Limits: no market prices for 2022 and no poll archive for 2024, so polls and markets are never compared in the same races. The House results file needs a registration form, so House fundamentals are not calibrated.

## A. How large are polling errors?

Error = poll average minus result, in points of margin. It is split into a part shared by all races of a cycle (the cycle bias) and a race part.

| Cycle | Senate bias | House-district bias | Generic-ballot error |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1998 | -2.3 | -2.8 | +2.3 |
| 2002 | +1.6 | +0.7 | +5.7 |
| 2006 | -3.0 | +0.5 | +6.1 |
| 2010 | -0.2 | +0.2 | +0.5 |
| 2014 | +3.3 | +3.4 | +3.3 |
| 2018 | -0.2 | -0.8 | -1.3 |
| 2022 | +2.0 | +0.3 | +2.1 |
| **Typical size (RMS)** | **2.1** | **1.7** | **3.6** |

Positive = polls too Democratic.

| Race part | Senate midterms | House districts | Model v1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Irreducible error (base) | 2.4 | 1.4 | 4.5 |
| Noise of one poll (sampling) | 9.7 | 9.4 | 7.0 |
| Total error, all races (RMS) | 6.6 | 8.0 | |
| Races | 213 | 630 | |

For comparison, all Senate cycles 1998–2022 including presidential years: base 2.9, sampling 8.9, typical cycle bias 3.1.
