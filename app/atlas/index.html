Our estimates and the what-if

Three signals carry our own estimate, with the arithmetic shown. Field probabilities multiply the two axis probabilities; the axes were chosen to be independent, so this is an approximation.

The what-if shifts reflect our judgment of how much each signal tells. They are combined as independent evidence on the log-odds scale and keep the average forecast unchanged.

Axis 1 · Do checks and balances hold?Measured as: V-Dem still rates the US a democracy (electoral or liberal) for 2030, in its Democracy Report 2031. In March 2026 V-Dem moved the US from liberal to electoral democracy, the first downgrade since 1969. AI forecast: 12 % for an autocracy rating, with the 2028 election as the main risk; so checks hold with 88 %.
Axis 2 · How fast does AI change the economy?Measured as: US labor productivity (BLS, nonfarm business) grows by at least 2.5 % a year on average from 2025 to 2030. It has grown 2.1 % a year since late 2019, about 3 % in 1995–2004 and about 1.5 % in 2007–2019. AI forecast: 34 %. Investment and adoption make a bull case, but gains usually take years to spread beyond tech.
